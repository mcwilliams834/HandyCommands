XAE and/or XAR?

1)	Read all steps first
2)	Make sure the TwinCAT 3.1 System Service (TcSysSrv) is stopped.  (Right Click My Computer, Manage, Services and Apps, Services)
3)	Uninstall TC3 from the Control Panel and uninstall any TC3 Functions
4)	Reboot.
5)	Delete this folder:  C:\TwinCAT\3.1
6)	In the Windows registry, delete these keys:
    a.	64bit
        i.	HKEY_LOCAL_MACHINE\SOFTWARE\Wow6432Node\Beckhoff\TwinCAT3
        ii.	HKEY_LOCAL_MACHINE\SOFTWARE\Wow6432Node\Beckhoff\TwinCAT3 Functions
    b.	32bit
        i.	HKEY_LOCAL_MACHINE\SOFTWARE\Beckhoff\TwinCAT3
        ii.	HKEY_LOCAL_MACHINE\SOFTWARE\Beckhoff\TwinCAT3 Functions
7)	Reboot.
8)	Install TC3.1 by right-clicking and choosing “run as administrator”.
9)	Choose “full install”
10)	Select the version(s) of Visual Studio for integration, you may select more than one.
11)	Reboot
